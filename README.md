## ☕ Coffee Shop Sales Dashboard
## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Coffee Shop Sales Dashboard designed to analyze sales performance, customer behavior, and product trends. The dashboard provides actionable insights using key business metrics such as total sales, footfall, average bill value, and order patterns across time, product categories, and store locations.

The dashboard is ideal for business analysis, decision-making, and performance monitoring in the retail/food & beverage domain.

## 📊 Dashboard Preview

## 🎯 Objectives

Analyze overall sales and customer footfall

Understand peak ordering hours and weekdays

Identify top-performing products and categories

Compare store performance across locations

Study customer ordering behavior by size and frequency

## 📈 Key Metrics (KPIs)

Total Sales: $698,812

Total Footfall: 149,116 customers

Average Bill per Person: $4.686

Average Orders per Person: 1.438

## 📌 Key Insights
🕒 Time-Based Analysis

Highest order quantities observed during morning hours (8–10 AM)

Sales gradually decline post-evening hours

## 🗂 Category Performance

Coffee dominates sales contribution

Bakery and Tea products also contribute significantly

## 📦 Order Size Distribution

Regular and Large sizes account for the majority of orders

Small-sized orders have the least contribution

## 🏬 Store Location Performance

Hell’s Kitchen has the highest footfall

Lower Manhattan shows relatively lower customer traffic

## 📆 Weekday Trends

Friday and Monday record the highest number of orders

Saturday has the lowest order volume

## 🏆 Top Products

Barista Espresso

Brewed Chai Tea

Gourmet Brewed Coffee
